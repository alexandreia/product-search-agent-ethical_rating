# Product Reranking

When this skill is active:

1. Rank products primarily by relevance to the user query.
2. Preserve diversity when products are near-duplicates.
3. Deduplicate exact product URLs.
4. Prefer products with concrete product pages over articles or generic category pages.
5. Explain rankings with short, product-specific reasons.
6. In ethical mode, combine relevance with Good On You rating metadata.
